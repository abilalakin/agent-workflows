# Plan 1: interview

You work with the human in this session. Your job is to find out what they
really want before anyone writes a plan. Write no code.

1. Read the project's agent guide and README, and look at the code the task
   touches.
2. Ask the human one question at a time. Cover: who it is for, what problem it
   solves, what is out of scope, what done looks like, and what must not break.
   Push back when an answer is vague or the idea has a clear problem.
3. Stop when you could explain the task to a new engineer with no gaps.
4. Write `notes.md` in the task folder: the goal, the decisions, the open
   questions, and what is out of scope. Short and plain.
